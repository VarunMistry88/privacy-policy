Privacy Policy

VmServices Mobile Applications

Developer: VmServices · Effective Date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VmServices ("we", "our", or "us") handles information across our mobile applications published on Google Play and the Apple App Store (including Daybreak Good Morning Messages and other applications published under the developer name VmServices, collectively "the Apps").

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Our Apps are designed with a local-first approach. We do not require account registration and we do not operate our own servers to collect or store your personal content. Any preferences, profiles, contacts, favorites, drafts, custom text, or media you choose to create or select inside an App are stored strictly locally on your device and are never uploaded to VmServices servers.

2. Device Permissions and Features

Depending on the features you use in an App, the App may request optional access to device capabilities:

  • Photos / Media Library (Optional): Accessed only when you pick a photo from your device to customize content or when you save exported images or wallpapers to your device gallery.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used solely to schedule local reminders on your device. No remote push servers or notification tokens are collected by VmServices.
  • Set Wallpaper (Android): Used only when you explicitly choose to apply a wallpaper to your Home Screen or Lock Screen.
  • Sharing: When you choose to share content, it is passed directly to the third-party app you select via your device's native share sheet.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Our Apps may use third-party SDKs and platform services that collect certain technical data automatically in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): Certain Apps display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, coarse location, app interaction data, and diagnostic information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. Where required by law (such as in the EEA and UK), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) requests your consent, and you can update your preferences at any time inside the App's privacy/ad settings.
  • Google Fonts: Typography files may be fetched over HTTPS from Google Fonts and cached locally on your device.
  • App Store / Google Play Services: In-app review prompts and distribution services are provided natively by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their respective privacy policies.

For details on how Google handles data, please review the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Data Sharing and Retention

VmServices does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. All content stored locally by an App remains on your device until you delete it inside the App, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Data processed by third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob is retained and managed under their respective policies.

5. Your Choices and Data Deletion

  • Delete Local Data: Remove saved items inside the App, clear the App's storage in your device settings, or uninstall the App at any time.
  • Manage Permissions: Revoke photo, media, or notification permissions at any time in your device's system settings.
  • Ad Privacy Controls: Update your consent choices inside the App (where available) and reset or limit your device's Advertising ID in your Android or iOS privacy settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect new features, apps, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated Effective Date.
